Server events are bounded (128 / three seconds), presentation-only, with stable
epoch/IDs and private outcomes. Short-lived events are emitted at the real
launch/collision/commitment resolution. No gameplay requests are manufactured to
produce sound. Rejected explicit requests may play quiet UI rejection, never a
successful cast. Cause IDs tie outcomes within the same locked action together
so rune/report/shrine confirmations suppress generic reward fanfares.

Visuals preserve the previous stable scenery and feedback implementation:
launch/impact marks, elemental colors, heal/ward marks, summon arrival, windup
danger zones, recovery distinction and rarity/completion marks. Optional marks
and particles are bounded; reduced effects preserve essential warnings. No prop
animation, persistent flash or opaque screen effect was added by this extension.

Music uses original editable notes and synthesized instruments, not Bfxr as a
music generator. Oakvale's refuge motif has a sparse Dunmere variation. Caravan's
road section transitions into refuge without replaying its intro on every poll.
Sustained combat engages music with a release hold; hidden/mute/disconnect/defeat
stop sources. Menus do not pause simulation. This is a small score, not a complete
bespoke soundtrack.

## Remaining gaps and deliberate restraint

- Families distinguish class/category; not every ability has an exclusive sample.
- No crossbow mechanic or separate physical bolt exists; none is invented.
- No critical-hit flag exists. Significant damage is presentation metadata only.
- Unarmored actors default to flesh; armor, structures and cover provide other
  material metadata. This is not anatomical or fully authored terrain audio.
- Dungeon transitions share restrained cues, not voice announcements. Continuous
  burn/bleed/summon hums and individual prop loops are deliberately absent.
- Held attacks intentionally drop some routine samples. A cold asset may be
  skipped after 250 ms; gameplay and visual information remain available.
- No realistic environment recordings or acoustic performances are included.
- Actual listening, extended fatigue review, physical mobile and Bluetooth latency
  remain unverified. Metrics cannot replace those checks. Detailed evidence:
  `reports/AUDIO_OVERHAUL_VALIDATION_2026-10-16.md`.
